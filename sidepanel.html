Data & privacy

Player identification runs on this device. Enabling the directory downloads public player profiles from Sleeper, at most once per 24 hours while you use the panel. Sleeper receives the request and your IP address, but not your search text.

Your favorite sources and directory are saved locally. The last highlighted name is kept only for the browser session, separately for each window. Clicking a research link sends that name to the selected website.

No account, analytics, browsing-history access, or paid PFF data. Directory source and terms.

Independent research companion. Not affiliated with PFF or the NFL.
